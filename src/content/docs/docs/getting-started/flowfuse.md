FlowFuse is the enterprise platform built around Node-RED, from the Node-RED team.
It is the managed path for running Node-RED in production, and the route most
organisations take when they need more than a single self-managed instance.

FlowFuse has been a sponsor of the Node-RED project since 2021.

## What it adds

On top of the Node-RED you already know, FlowFuse provides:

- Authentication and role-based access for your team
- Team workspaces, so several people can develop against the same flows
- Deployment pipelines for promoting flows from development to production
- Remote deployment and management of edge devices
- A hardened runtime, with backups and version history
- Professional support for both Node-RED and FlowFuse

## FlowFuse Cloud

The quickest way to start. Sign up, click **Create Instance**, and you have a
deploy-ready Node-RED editor in seconds, with no CLI and nothing to install.

A [free trial](https://app.flowfuse.com/account/create) is available.

## Self-hosting FlowFuse

FlowFuse can also run on your own infrastructure, locally, under Docker, or on
Kubernetes. See the FlowFuse
[installation instructions](https://flowfuse.com/docs/install/) for the
requirements and setup steps for each.
